How these numbers work (read this)

Football Manager doesn’t publish how its AI values players, so none of these are the game’s formulas. They’re rules of thumb drawn from community guides, meant as starting points you adjust using the in-game responses (agent reactions, “suggest terms”, club replies). I couldn’t run FM26 here, so nothing has been tested in a match.

  • Fee: shorter contract, older age, fringe role and a cash-strapped seller lower the expected price; key-player status and an early window raise it.
  • Wages: bands are multiples of your own squad average, capped by your top earner.
  • Signing-on ≤ one year of wages and agent fee ≈ 10% of the transfer fee are community habits, not game rules.
  • Role attribute lists are the classic FM role emphasis; check the role description in-game, since FM26 may have changed some.
