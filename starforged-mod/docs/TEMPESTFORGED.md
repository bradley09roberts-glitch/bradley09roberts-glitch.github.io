# Tempestforged design sheet (tier 4 - Stormreach)

Taken from the concept boards (Bestiary, Armory, Block Index, Places). This is the target for the 1.3.0 build.
The verbs this tier adds: **air dashes** and **conducting lightning**.

## Blocks
- **Natural:** Stormstone, Skyrock, Skysoil, Stormgrass, Stormwood Log, Stormwood Planks, Stormleaves.
- **Resources:** Aetherium Ore, Block of Aetherium, Charged Aetherium Block, Thunder Crystal Cluster.
  Aetherium must be **charged by lightning** before it can be used.
- **Building:** Tempest Bricks, Chiseled Tempest Bricks, Aetherglass, Storm Lantern, Wind Chime.
- **Functional:** Storm Dynamo, Aetherium Conductor, Storm Relay, Storm Capacitor, Wind Vent, Gale Vent, Shock Plate,
  Thunder Rune, Gale Rune, Sky Anchor, Tempest Altar, Stormgate Core, Lightning Beacon, Weather Engine.
- **Citadel / puzzle:** Citadel Core, Rotating Conductor, Splitter Relay, Overload Relay, Tempest Seal, Storm Lift,
  Cyclone Emitter.

## Boss: Veyr
- Entrance: his armour assembles from lightning strikes.
- Phase 2: redirect his own lightning into him using the conductors.
- Phase 3: fight across floating fragments; Tornado Wall.
- Final attack: The Last Thunder (keep moving between the travelling Eyes of the Storm).
