# Starforged roadmap

Brainstormed design for turning Starforged into the central progression mod of a pack.
Principle: **every tier adds a new verb, not just bigger numbers.**
Star = cosmic powers, Sun = elemental aggression, Moon = gravity, Storm = movement, Void = mastering everything together.

## Main campaign
| Tier | Name | Dimension | Verb | Boss | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Starforged | Overworld | cosmic powers | Eclipse Sovereign | Done (1.0) |
| 2 | Sunforged | Sunlands | fire / elemental | Sun Warden | Done (1.1) |
| 3 | Moonforged | The Pale Reach | low gravity, tides | The Pale Matriarch (1100 HP) | Planned |
| 4 | Tempestforged | Stormreach | wind, lightning, air dashes | Veyr, the Tempest Regent (1300 HP) | Planned |
| Finale | Voidforged | The Event Horizon | everything combined | Asterion, the Unmade | Planned |

## Tier 3 - Moonforged (The Pale Reach)
- **Low gravity:** about 3x jump height, slow falls, big knockback, longer projectile range.
- **Tide cycle (~10 min):**
  - High Tide: weaker gravity, Silver Seas rise, moon mobs get stronger.
  - Low Tide: the seas retreat and expose caves, ruins and buried treasure.
  - Some resources can only be gathered at one tide.
- **Biomes:**
  - Regolith Flats
  - Silver Seas
  - Selenite Hollows
  - The Far Side (no light at all)
  - Shattered Rim (floating islands, built for mobility gear)
- **Moonsilver** (needs a Sunsteel pickaxe, 3200 durability). Its strength is control, not raw damage.
- **Moonsilver armor:**
  - Lunar Ward: a shield that absorbs one heavy hit every 30 s.
  - Gravity Shift: sneak + jump toggles your own gravity.
  - High Tide bonuses.
- **Weapons:**
  - Crescent Glaive: orbit throw; Gravity Nail pins nearby enemies.
  - Orrery Staff: three orbiting moons; Moonfall combines them.
  - Phase Daggers: dash through enemies, the cuts detonate together.
  - Moonshot Crossbow: bolts ricochet; Gravity Beacon pulls mobs in.
  - Stasis Bell: freezes time for 4 s.
  - Tether Hook: grapple that swings you in low gravity.
- **Mobs:**
  - Regolith Skimmer (swims under the dust)
  - Lunar Moth (eats light)
  - Selenite Sentinel (reflects projectiles)
  - Umbral Lurker (only moves when you're not looking)
- **Pet:** Moonkit (finds ores and treasure). **Mount:** Moonleaper (charged leaps).
- **Structure: Tidal Orrery.** Tide-dependent halls, gravity-inversion corridors, a Bell Tower and the Orrery Alignment ring puzzle.
- **Boss: The Pale Matriarch.**
  - Entrance: an eclipse, and the sea lifts into a sphere.
  - Phases:
    - Waxing Moon
    - Inversion (break 3 Lunar Anchors)
    - New Moon (4 copies; only the real one casts a shadow, so light helps and the Helios Scepter is a cross-tier advantage)
    - Moonfall (hide under anchor wreckage or kill her in time)
  - Drops: Heart of the Moon, Tidecaller Glaive, Crown of Tides.

## Tier 4 - Tempestforged (Stormreach)
- **Getting there:** craft a Skybreaker Core and activate it on top of a Tidal Orrery at High Tide.
- **Biomes:**
  - Thunderhead Steppe
  - Shardwind Cliffs (side winds)
  - Static Grove
  - Stormgrave
  - Endless Eye
- **Aetherium:** must be charged by lightning before use.
- **Aetherium armor:** Stormstep gives 3 air dashes; also lightning immunity and less knockback.
- **Weapons:**
  - Skybreaker Halberd: launch up, then slam down with lightning.
  - Tempest Javelin: becomes a lightning rod; recall it through enemies.
  - Gale Blades: send out a tornado.
  - Stormhook
  - Arc Cannon: charged beam.
- **Mobs:**
  - Static Wisp
  - Shardwing
  - Stormbound
  - Thunderjaw
- **Pet:** Zephyr Sprite. **Mount:** Storm Roc (the fastest).
- **Structure: Tempest Citadel.** A Conductor Network puzzle: route Sky → Rod → Conductor → Relay → Core in four wings.
- **Boss: Veyr.**
  - Entrance: his armour assembles from lightning strikes.
  - Phase 2: redirect his own lightning into him using the conductors.
  - Phase 3: fight across floating fragments.
  - Final attack: The Last Thunder (keep moving between the travelling Eyes of the Storm).
  - Drops: Stormheart, Skycleaver, Tempest Crown.

## Finale - Voidforged (The Event Horizon)
- **Story:** each boss guarded part of a prison. The Last Watcher steered you into breaking every seal so that someone strong enough would exist to kill what's inside. Orin the Astronomer is your NPC ally.
- **Celestial Key:** made from the four boss hearts at the restored Observatory, triggering the Total Convergence sky event.
- **The Event Horizon:**
  - Fragments of every earlier world.
  - Time-slip zones (slow, fast, frozen).
  - Echo Fields replaying past fights.
- **Celestium:** fused from every earlier material at the Celestial Forge, not mined.
  - Armor has a Cosmic Cycle that switches between Star, Sun, Moon and Storm modes.
- **Boss: Asterion, the Unmade.**
  - Phases: Star, Sun (an imploding supernova), Moon (shadow copies), Storm.
  - Final phase, First Light: reignite the First Star using the Celestial Pillars.
- **True ending:** new constellations appear in the telescope and the "Starforged" advancement unlocks.
  - Ending choice: Reseal (calmer world) or Consume (harder postgame).

## Systems
1. **Watcher's Codex:** progression book with tabs for Journey, Creatures, Events, Relics, Structures, Constellations, Lore, Challenges and Bosses. Unknown entries show as ???. Milestones are adventure-style, not grind.
2. **Observatory restoration:** claim it after the Astral Golem, then upgrade it:
   - Workshop
   - Telescope (forecasts events)
   - Planetarium (fast travel)
   - Trophy Hall
   - Celestial Forge
3. **Constellation Engraving:** connect Star Chart points at the Celestial Anvil.
   - Engravings: Hunter, Serpent, Anchor, Comet, Lantern, Phoenix, Tempest.
   - Slots by tier: 1, 2, 2, 3, 4.
4. **Weapon Resonance → Awakening:** each weapon charges by using its own mechanic.
   - Examples: Awakened Starcaller, Cometbreaker, Solar Orbit chakram, Paradox Daggers.
5. **Fusion weapons:**
   - Equinox Blade (Eclipse + Flare)
   - Cosmos Scepter (Starcaller + Helios)
   - Worldbreaker (Meteor Hammer + Skybreaker)
6. **Solar Tempering:** a heat-gauge timing minigame for Sunsteel gear.
7. **Mini-bosses:**
   - Fallen Titan (climbable Starfall titan)
   - Mimic Monarch (shell game)
   - Void Hunter (stalks you after you kill many Void Stalkers)
   - Ashen Champion (punishes cheesing)
   - Caldera Wyrm (bait its lunge)
   - Dune Behemoth
   - Thunderjaw Alpha
8. **World events:**
   - Aurora Night
   - Comet Approach (3-night countdown, then a crash-site dungeon)
   - Void Rift Night
   - Solar Eclipse
   - Solar Flare (shade matters)
   - Ashstorm
   - Phoenix Migration
   - Blood Moon Tide
   - Meteor Eclipse
   - Supercell
9. **Endgame:**
   - Starfall Rifts: procedural depth dungeons with modifiers and a deepest-depth tracker.
   - Ascended bosses: changed mechanics, not just more HP.
   - Orin's Bounty Board
   - New Game+ "Next Cycle"
10. **Hybrid set bonuses:**
    - Twilight
    - Nightfall
    - Eclipsed Sun
    - Thunderfire
    - Skydancer
    - Falling Sky
11. **Celestial Harness:** mount modules:
    - Cargo
    - Solar Engine
    - Lunar Saddle
    - Tempest Wings
    - Armour Plate
    - Recall Sigil

## Suggested build order
1. Watcher's Codex
2. Observatory restoration
3. Constellation Engraving
4. Resonance / Awakening
5. Mini-bosses: Fallen Titan, Ashen Champion, Caldera Wyrm
6. Events: Aurora, Comet, Void Rift, Solar Flare
7. Ascended Sovereign and Ascended Warden
8. Moonforged
9. Starfall Rifts
10. Tempestforged
11. Fusion / Celestium
12. Event Horizon and Asterion
13. Next Cycle
