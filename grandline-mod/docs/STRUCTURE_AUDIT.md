# Structure audit ledger

This file is generated by `tools/gen_structure_audit.py` from the structure dumps taken before and after the building overhaul (`GL_SELFTEST=structures`), plus the written reviews in `docs/audit/`.

**What each row records:** every unique structure the mod generates, with its footprint, block counts, height and the new kit blocks it uses. "Anchors" are the gameplay hooks: NPC spawn roles, boards, cells, quest anchors, treasure and secret markers, and loot chests by table. "Preserved" checks that nothing a system depends on was lost.

**How variants are covered:**

- Settlement buildings are listed once per type × architectural style: 22 types in 15 styles.
- Plot rotation and terrain are exercised by `LandmarkCheck`, which tests every rotation, and by the real generated towns (`GL_DUMP_TOWNS`).

## Streets, harbours and town composition

Before: every building's door was joined to the town centre by its own straight 2-wide diagonal path (a star of spokes), and the dock was a plain 5-wide spruce pier with fence posts. After: streets form a minimum spanning tree over the centre, every door and the dock root, drawn as axis-aligned L-shaped streets (the elbow chosen to cross fewer plots); the street from the centre to the harbour is 3 wide; paving mixes two materials per style; lamp posts in the island's style stand every 12 blocks, alternating sides, only on open ground. Piers are dock planks on weathered-beam pilings with a 9-wide T-head where ships tie up (dock tip unchanged), mooring bollards, lamp posts, a ladder down to the water and cargo waiting on the head. Jungle buildings stand on stilts with steps to the door. Plot positions are unchanged, so towns keep their layout and every system that refers to a building still finds it.
