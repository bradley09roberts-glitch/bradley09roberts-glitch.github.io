# Controls

Every key below can be changed in **Options → Controls → Key Binds** (category *Grand Line Odyssey*). In game,
**/controls** (or the **Controls** button on the Character screen, `C`) shows this list with the keys you actually
have bound, plus the fight loop and how to read a foe.

## Fighting

| Action | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Strike | tap Left mouse | Works on anyone you are looking at, in or out of Combat Mode. Quick blows chain into a short combo. |
| Heavy blow | hold Left mouse, release | Slower and stronger the longer you hold (up to 1.5 s); breaks a guard. |
| Combat Mode | `R` | Turns on guard, techniques on 1–9 and lock-on. Turn it off to use your hotbar normally. |
| Guard | hold Right mouse (Combat Mode) | Takes a quarter of the damage and costs stamina per blow. Start guarding just before a blow lands to **parry**: no damage and the attacker staggers. |
| Dodge | `Left Alt` + a direction | A quick step with a split second where nothing touches you. Costs stamina; short cooldown. |
| Techniques | `1`–`9` (Combat Mode) | Each has its own cooldown, shown on the bar. |
| Next technique set | `Tab` or mouse wheel (Combat Mode) | Martial, fruit, Haki and race sets. |
| Lock on | Middle mouse (Combat Mode) | Picks the foe you face; only someone you can see. The lock drops a second after they go out of sight. |
| Observation / Armament / Conqueror's Haki | `H` / `J` / `K` | Once unlocked. |
| Zoan transformation | `G` | Zoan fruit users. |

Blows go only where they can reach: walls, closed doors, floors and other solid blocks stop them, for you and for
every foe. Partial cover (a low wall, a fence) only protects what it covers. If a blow is stopped you will see a
spark on the wall and "Blocked by cover".

## The fight loop

1. Close in and pick a target (look at it, or lock on).
2. Strike: tap for quick blows, hold for a heavy one.
3. Watch the foe: a **red flash over its head** means a heavy blow in about half a second; a **gold ring at its
   feet** means a technique; **marks on the ground** show where a boss attack will land.
4. Guard (just before the blow = parry) or dodge out of the way.
5. Hit back while it recovers; save your techniques for openings.

The **target frame** (top left) shows who you are facing: rank mark (♛ boss, ◆ elite, · minion) and name, role and
faction, health, how they stand towards you (Your crew, Friendly, Neutral, Suspicious, Hostile) and how dangerous
they are for you (▽ Weaker, = Matched, ▲ Dangerous, ▲▲ Overwhelming). A foe that is dangerous for you warns you over
the hotbar before its first blow.

Practice any time with **/spar** (or ask an elder or a master): a sparring partner fights you without hurting you.
