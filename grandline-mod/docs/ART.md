# Art sources

Most textures, models and animations are generated by code (`tools/gen_assets.py`, `tools/gen_animations.py`).
Painted art was generated with **Higgsfield** (model GPT Image 2.5, medium quality) on 2026-10-12 and processed by
hand-written scripts (crop to the target aspect, resize, PNG). Prompts asked for original scenes only: no
characters, logos or text other than the poster/logo lettering.
