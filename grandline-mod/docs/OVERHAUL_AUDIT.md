# Gameplay overhaul — audit ledger

Scope: the playtest report "combat is confusing; quests feel generic; some enemies are much too strong; attacks hit
through walls; hard to tell who characters are, their faction and their danger; recruitable crew wanted; systems
shallow or unfinished". World, islands, buildings, blocks, assets and shaders are preserved.

**Reversible checkpoint:** commit `205b415` (release 0.2.0) is the last commit before the overhaul. Every overhaul
change is on top of it on branch `claude/friendly-darwin-gv73pq`; `git checkout 205b415` (or the 0.2.0 jar in
`dist/`) is the way back.

Complete registries (every enemy archetype with its rank and tuned numbers, every story boss, every ability, every
quest line) are in [audit/overhaul-registries.md](audit/overhaul-registries.md), generated by the self-test.

## How the player journey was traced

New world → character creation → starter isle (Shiokaze Isle for East Blue) → the elder's first quest → bandits in
the wilds → the next island (Shells Town) → its arc and boss → recruitment → the East Blue chain. At each step the
code path was read (combat input → `CombatManager` → `Hits`; NPC AI → `NpcCombatGoal`; spawns → `Encounters` /
`PopulationManager`; quests → `Quests`/`QuestRegistry`/`StoryQuests`; dialogue → `Dialogues`/`NotableHooks`; crew →
`CrewManager`) and the numbers were measured with a reference character in the self-test.

## Findings and fixes

Severity: **S1** breaks fairness or progress; **S2** confusing or frustrating; **S3** polish.

### Combat correctness (line of effect)

| # | Sev | Finding | Root cause | Fix | Verified by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | S1 | Blows landed through walls, doors and floors | `Hits.cone/sphere/line` picked victims by distance and angle only; `Hits.hit` had no line check | `combat/Cover`: rays through real block collision shapes from where a blow comes from to several points of the victim's body (centre, head, nearest point, feet, flanks of big bodies); the gate in `Hits.hit` for every blow (328 call sites), cover-aware pickers | overhaul self-test: open ground, stone wall, closed/open door, corner, waist-high cover, glass pane, floor between levels |
| C2 | S1 | NPCs swung at and hurt players through walls; used techniques with no line | `NpcCombatGoal` attacked whenever within reach, techniques on a timer | strikes and techniques only with a reachable target; the technique is re-checked when it fires | "an NPC never hurts a target it cannot reach" |
| C3 | S1 | Lock-on and ability targets could be chosen through walls | client lock picked by angle; server trusted the id | lock and technique targets must be in sight (client and server); the lock drops 1 s after losing sight | "sight for lock-on is blocked by walls" |
| C4 | S1 | A piercing shot could tunnel through a wall after its first victim; wide shots hit round corners | the projectile moved its full step after an entity hit; the wide sweep had no line check | swept flight: the step is cut at the first block, everything before it is hit in order, the wall is struck; wide sweep needs a line from the path | projectile lanes: open, wall, fast shot at a pane, pierce then wall |
| C5 | S1 | Explosions, shockwaves, beams and Buster Call shells hurt people behind walls | area pickers had no line check; beams were not cut at walls | blasts need a line from their centre; beams stop at the first block; Buster Call shells too | "a blast reaches bodies in the open and not those behind a wall", "a beam ... stops there" |
| C6 | S2 | Named characters' name tags showed through walls | always-visible custom names render see-through | name tags only for the crosshair target or someone in plain sight | client capture |
| C7 | S3 | Selection cache could let a blow pass if cover changed in the same tick | cache kept for two ticks | same-tick only | cover self-test (rebuilt within a tick) |

Narrow exceptions (documented on the hit with `Hits.Info.asPenetrating()`): boss arena hazard zones hurt whoever stands
in them (lava floors, poison mists). Nothing else is exempt.

### Fairness, AI and balance

| # | Sev | Finding | Root cause | Fix | Verified by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | S1 | Early bandits took ~40 % of a new character's health per blow, a heavy blow nearly all of it | NPC blow = `2 + 1.2 × tier` + full weapon damage (+5 for a cutlass); 0.2.0's 0.45 multiplier was a stop-gap | `balance/Tuning`: blows from rank and sea tier (minion 9 %, standard 11 %, elite 12 % of the reference health per light blow), per-blow caps | measured table (BALANCE.md) |
| B2 | S1 | Story bosses far stronger than their island (Gallowgate: lore tier 3 on a tier-1 island) | numbers came from the lore tier | bosses fight one tier above their island; health = 45 reference blows | registries (story bosses) |
| B3 | S2 | Vanilla difficulty stacked on the mod's numbers (Easy halved, Hard ×1.5) | damage types scaled "when caused by living non-player" | damage types never scale; the mod's difficulty follows the world difficulty by default | config + table |
| B4 | S2 | Unarmed fighters hit for ~1 at level 1 | fists had no weapon bonus | trained fists count as a common blade (+5) | — |
| B5 | S2 | NPC blows got a hidden mastery bonus on top of tier | `masteryLvl = tier × 8` for NPCs | removed (tier is already in the tuning) | measured table |
| B6 | S1 | A crowd of foes could chain a player down; stagger after stagger | no limit on melee attackers; stuns could stack back to back | melee slots (two at once on Standard), rotation after three swings; 2 s stagger immunity after a stagger; a player held ≤ 1.5 s | "at most two press a melee attack", "a stagger ... cannot be chained" |
| B7 | S1 | One unannounced blow could take a large share of a player's health | no cap | per-blow caps by rank (22–40 %), 55 % for announced boss attacks | "no single unannounced blow ..." |
| A1 | S2 | Foes chased forever after losing sight (48 blocks) | no memory rule | search where last seen; give up after 8 s; 10 s calm towards that player | "an NPC that lost sight gives up" |
| A2 | S2 | Foes ground against walls they could not get round | no progress check | give up after ~8 s without progress; settlement folk leashed to 64 blocks from home | same |
| A3 | S2 | Wild gangs could appear on ponds, inside blocks, or at a town's edge | spawn point only checked height | no fluids, no collisions, never inside a town | code review (spawn rules) |
| A4 | S2 | Being attacked while talking to someone | no rule | no new fight is started with a player in a conversation | code review |
| A5 | S2 | Foes' heavy blows and techniques came without warning | no tell | heavy: red flash and 0.6 s wind-up; technique: gold ring and 0.6 s; danger warning before the first blow | client capture |
