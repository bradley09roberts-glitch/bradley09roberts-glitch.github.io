# NFL Props Lab: technical audit, November 2026

RESEARCH ONLY. This record covers forecasts and rules tested against US bookmaker prices that a UK
customer cannot take. Nothing in it is a bet, a tip or a promise of profit.

- **Date and scope:** 3 November 2026. The NBA model (N1), the NFL model (J1, M and their blend X1),
  the odds and money code, data handling in both sports, and the paper-tracked rule. Decision
  record: D-044 in DECISIONS.md. Code: commits ad80b68 and bcae4fd (src/npl/books.py, track.py,
  picks.py, nba/appdata.py, nba/live.py, nba/calib.py; the n12_audit backtest).
- **Data:** stored pre-game snapshots from The Odds API (us region, core markets, main lines): NBA
  2024-25 (1,230 games, 71,922 scored props), the NBA 2025-26 sample (every fourth date: 42 dates,
  302 games, 18,753 scored props) and NFL 2023 (284 games, 10,315 scored props). Raw quotes and the
  audit's scripts and outputs stay out of git (they contain prices); this record holds aggregates.
- **Notation:** [a, b] is a 95% block-bootstrap interval. "Fit" is 2024-25 (NFL: weeks 1-11) and
  "test" the 2025-26 sample (NFL: weeks 12-22). Log-loss changes are nats per prop; negative is
  better. ROI is flat 1 unit at the stated price. EV = probability x decimal price - 1.
- **Verification status** is as recorded in the workflow journal at about 01:35 UTC on 3 November
  2026. "Verification pending" means no verifier result had been recorded by then.

## 1. Summary

- **What was audited.** Stage 1: online research and code audits of the odds and money code, N1's
  mathematics, the NFL model and data handling. Stage 2: each proposed fix or rule tested once under
  a frozen protocol in four tracks (market rules, injury-report absences, N1 maths, NFL), then
  rebuilt from scratch by an independent verifier whose job was to knock it down.
- **The arithmetic is sound.** The stored rows rebuild from the raw quotes exactly, and EV, ROI,
  settlement, voids, margin removal, bootstrap intervals and calibration all check out (section 3).
- **The market definitions were loose.** The "best US price" was the highest quote from any
  us-region book, so offshore books, ladder rungs and price pairs implying an arbitrage set it. The
  consensus line was a mode over quote rows, so books hanging ladders (and, in the 2025-26 research
  rows, 4,590 duplicate rows) outvoted other books, biasing the line low on 1.8-2.9% of NBA props.
- **The tracked rule had never been backtested as coded.** 27.5% of its 2025-26 bets were priced
  only at an offshore book through its fallback (best US price where BetMGM did not quote the
  line). At regulated prices the fallback leg lost in both seasons (-8.5% on 283 bets; -9.3% on 67).
- **No paper record existed.** Nothing settled the tracked props or measured closing-line value
  (CLV), and D-043's checkpoint was wrong: a 2% edge needs about 14,000 independent bets, not 3,000,
  to show in profit with 80% power.
- **Fixed (D-044):** an append-only paper record with settlement, slate-block ROI intervals and CLV
  (`npl track`); best prices from regulated US books' main lines only; one vote per book; an
  arbitrage flag; voids and pushes out of turnover; pushes graded, missing stats left open; nba-0.3
  live; and the tracked rule narrowed to BetMGM's own price before its first recorded run.
- **N1 (NBA).** Its lean to unders is mostly a mean bias for players with a posted line, and its
  overconfidence is noise in its mean, not distributions that are too narrow. Three verified fixes
  now run live as nba-0.3: a better standalone forecaster that adds nothing beyond the market.
  Official injury reports make it better still, but the 3-hour market has already priced them.
- **NFL.** Its lean to unders is mostly two calculation errors (the starting QB's start probability;
  usage shares shrunk too hard). Corrected, it only ties the market; decision pending verification.
- **Verification.** Of the nine Stage 2 verifications completed, seven returned "overstated" (the
  numbers reproduced; the conclusions went too far) and two "confirmed" (line-row rate factors and
  NB1 points, neither of which changes a priced probability). Two NFL checks were still running.
- **Bottom line.** No rule or model shows an edge that survives correction for multiple testing on
  a clean holdout, and there is none yet: the 2025-26 sample has been examined many times. The
  de-vigged market consensus beats or ties every model tested. Paper tracking, judged first by CLV,
  is the next test. All prices are US references a UK customer cannot take; nothing promises profit.

## 2. Protocol

- **NBA.** Fit on 2024-25 (season key 2025; 164 dates, 71,922 props); test once on the 2025-26
  sample (every fourth date: 42 dates, 18,753 props). That sample is not pristine: it was n11's test
  set, and Stage 1 audits used it, in some cases on the very idea later tested (BetMGM against the
  other books, off-consensus lines). The 2026-27 live season is the next clean holdout.
- **NFL.** Fit on 2023 weeks 1-11 (6,025 props); test on weeks 12-22 (4,290 props, 118 games, 11
  week blocks). exp13 and Stage 1 had examined the whole season and X1's stacking weights were
  chosen on 2023; both biases favour the tweaks.
- **Prices.** One stored snapshot per game, about 3 hours before tip-off (NBA) and 2 hours before
  kick-off (NFL); no closing snapshot. Every stored line is a half-point, so there are no pushes; a
  player who did not play is void.
- **Frozen choices.** Every parameter, threshold and book set is fixed on fit data (market-rule
  thresholds from a 2024-25 grid, at least 300 bets); test data are scored once; reruns are disclosed.
- **Scoring.** Log loss at the real line, mean P(over) minus the over rate, calibration slope, CRPS on
  all played player-games (to catch "calibration by widening") and flat-stake ROI at a named price.
- **Intervals and multiplicity.** Date-block bootstrap (NBA; 2,000-10,000 resamples) and week-block
  (NFL), cross-checked with game, player and cluster-t intervals. Families are corrected with Holm,
  White's Reality Check (also studentised) and, where looks were counted, Bonferroni.
- **Adversarial verification.** Each claimed improvement (adopt or lead, plus selected audit issues)
  is rebuilt from the claim's text by a separate verifier with its own code, a read-only database and
  frozen copies of the code at fc92cd5. Verdicts: confirmed, overstated, refuted or uncertain.

## 3. Calculations checked and found correct

**Data and timing**
- n10 consensus() on the stored quotes rebuilds every column of the NBA 2024-25 and 2025-26 rows and
  the NFL exp13 rows exactly, and recomputed N1 P(over) (P(X >= floor(line)+1)) matches to 3e-16.
  In 120 random raw responses parsing is exact; every stored row is a main line.
- Snapshots fall on the provider's 5-minute grid; all 1,815 are 2.41-4.07 h (NBA) or 2.07-2.41 h
  (NFL) before the start, so there are no in-play prices; quote age has median 0 and 99th
  percentile at most 8 minutes.
- No player mislinks in NFL 2023 or NBA 2024-26 (names agree on all 69,747 linked NBA points props);
  as-of joins are strictly before tip-off, with no leakage in player, team or opponent states.

**Margin removal and consensus**
- Multiplicative v additive, power, Shin and odds-ratio de-vig: market log loss 0.68556 v
  0.68549-0.68550 (2024-25); the over-lean survives every method; dropping offshore books makes the
  market worse (+0.0002 to +0.0003); and "the market beats N1" holds under every odds choice tested
  (gap 0.0195-0.0197 in 2024-25; interval of the difference [0.0163, 0.0228]).
- D-044 parity check: the shipped books.py consensus matches the verifier's independent corrected
  consensus on 100% of props in both NBA seasons (73,488 and 19,327 props) for the line, BetMGM
  pricing, best regulated price and arbitrage flag. The fair price differs only because it averages
  every two-way price at the line (mean absolute difference 0.0003 and 0.0007).

**Money, settlement and intervals**
- EV and ROI formulas (picks, the n10 and n11 profit columns, money.py's expected return, fair price
  and settlement, including 5% exchange commission) are correct.
- Voids follow book rules; no whole-number consensus line exists in any stored season (0 of about
  103,000), so no pushes enter the results. Re-settling from canon (PRA = points + rebounds +
  assists) matches the stored actuals on 100% of rows, with no missing stats among 302,372 played
  NBA rows and no duplicate player-games.
- Bootstrap intervals are stable from 1,000 to 10,000 resamples, and date, game and NFL week
  clusters agree. calib.py's test log losses reproduce (0.68815 recalibrated, 0.68695 anchored,
  0.68723 market, 0.70578 raw N1).
- picks.py (fc92cd5) and track.py (ad80b68) do what their docstrings say for half-point lines: the
  BetMGM pair is complete, the higher-EV side is chosen, and voids, PRA, CLV (price x margin-free
  later consensus - 1, at least two books) and the slate-block interval are right. The D-008 record
  and the NBA settlement count no edition published at or after the start.

**N1 mathematics**
- The negative binomial pmf, tail folding, the minutes residual table (equal to a 2018-23 refit to
  0.002), PRA on every other minute and band steps are numerically harmless at every real line. Pace
  elasticity is 0.78-1.12, league levels are concurrent, and P(plays) never enters P(over).
- Widths are right on prop rows (squared error over model variance 0.97-1.05; PIT tails 9-12%), and,
  fed the market's own component prices, N1's PRA convolution prices PRA as well as the market does
  (0.69243 v 0.69238 in 2024-25; 0.69196 v 0.69209 in 2025-26).

**NFL**
- The harnesses reproduce exp13's J1 and M exactly, and X1 equals the per-market linear pool of the
  two. Simulated team volumes are unbiased in 2023 (plays 63.20 v 63.15; pass attempts 33.81 v
  33.66); only scrambles run low (1.76 v 1.92).
- Widths on prop rows are about right (PIT variance x 12 of 0.88-1.04); as in the NBA, the location
  bias is specific to players with props. The exp13 row build, its voids and the injury-report
  P(plays) constants all reproduce.

**Stage 2 reproduction.** Every verifier rebuilt the tester's bet counts, ROIs and log losses with
independent code and found them exact, with no outcome leakage; the disagreements are about inference.

## 5. Online research findings

References are listed with their URLs under Sources; "stored lines" marks the lab's own data.

**De-vig methods**
- For a two-way market, Shin's method, the additive method and Buchdahl's odds-proportional weights
  give identical probabilities, so the choice is multiplicative, additive, power or odds-ratio
  (implied package; Clarke et al. 2017). "Worst case" is a conservative filter, not a probability
  model; on the stored lines it removed about 20-25% of +EV flags with no change in results.
- Work favouring power or Shin (Clarke et al. 2017; Strumbelj 2014) comes from markets with a wide
  spread of prices. On near-even props the method hardly matters: multiplicative and power differ
  by a median 0.28 points per prop, and log-loss differences are at most 0.0002 and change sign
  between samples. Keep multiplicative.
- The bias that matters is overs: in all three stored samples the over wins 0.5-1.0 points less
  often than the margin-free price says (0.4893 v 0.4946; 0.4845 v 0.4949; NFL 0.4949 v 0.5013),
  consistent with bettors' preference for overs (Levitt 2004; Paul and Weinbach). No symmetric
  de-vig removes it, and a logit shift fitted on 2024-25 improved 2025-26 log loss by -0.00016
  [-0.00039, +0.00006], not significantly.

**Market efficiency on props**
- No peer-reviewed test of over/under, alternate-line or same-game-parlay mispricing in NBA or NFL
  player props was found; most "props are soft" content is affiliate or vendor marketing. For sides
  and totals, books shade towards public biases by about half a point of win probability, too little
  to pay a bettor after the margin (Levitt 2004); opening lines carry the errors, including NBA
  absences, and the close removes most of them (Dare, Dennis and Paul 2015; Moskowitz 2021).
- Stored lines: betting every under loses at every price. NBA 2024-25 -2.91% [-3.94, -1.94] at the
  best US price and -5.26% [-6.25, -4.29] at BetMGM; 2025-26 -1.74% [-3.98, +0.41] and -4.80%
  [-7.22, -2.51]; NFL 2023 -2.42% [-4.56, -0.42] and -4.92% [-7.48, -2.63].
- The 3-hour market prices uncertain absences correctly on average (section 6.2). After the
  snapshot, 2.1% of players with a prop did not play, and teammates of a late scratch with a points
  line of 12 or more went over 53.2% v a fair 49.3% (+3.9 points [2.0, 5.6]): a speed edge, not a
  model edge. Trading on non-public status is illegal (October 2025 federal indictment).
- Integrity rules shrink the menu: no unders on two-way and 10-day players at partner books since
  2024, and since 16 October 2026 no same-player under parlays or unders on players expected to play
  under 12 minutes. Parlays hold about 17-31%, against single-digit holds on single bets.

**Book accuracy, shared feeds and soft books**
- Practitioners find no truly sharp book for props (Establish The Run); Pikkit's line-leadership
  ranking put FanDuel first for NBA props and Pinnacle first for NFL props. Sharpness is best measured
  by cross-book expected v realised ROI and proper scoring rules (DataGolf). Pinnacle, Circa,
  BookMaker and Novig are not in the data, so the lab's market is a consensus of mostly retail books.
- Stored lines, each book's margin-free price v the consensus of the others (log loss; positive is
  worse), NBA 2024-25: BetOnline -0.00022 [-0.00038, -0.00006] (best), FanDuel -0.00002
  [-0.00032, +0.00028], DraftKings +0.00026 [+0.00002, +0.00051], Caesars +0.00026
  [+0.00003, +0.00048] and BetMGM +0.00092 [+0.00066, +0.00120] (worst); NFL 2023 BetMGM
  +0.00094 [+0.00038, +0.00156]; 2025-26 separates only Bovada (+0.00094 [+0.00007, +0.00181]).
  When BetMGM disagrees with the others, the others tend to be right (weight on its
  deviation -0.38 [-0.64, -0.06]; NFL -0.79 [-1.61, -0.07]).
- Equal weights do as well as fitted ones (+0.00005 [-0.00006, +0.00016] on 2025-26), but some books
  share a feed (BetRivers and Unibet US identical on 98.5% of NFL 2023 props; Huddle supplies one
  simulated feed to many operators), so an equal-weight average over-counts some opinions. A FanDuel
  line below the consensus predicts fewer overs (-2.65 points [-4.51, -0.66]); as a rule (R6) the
  signal is smaller than the margin.
- BetMGM, the least accurate book in every stored dataset, is a plausible soft target, and soft
  targets are where winning accounts get limited. Betting it against the other books looked
  profitable in 2024-25 and did not confirm in 2025-26 (868 bets +8.1% [+1.8, +14.6] v 381 bets
  +2.1% [-9.6, +14.3] at EV above 1%); for R2 see section 6.1.

**Line shopping and +EV screening**
- Line shopping is the most reliable lever: average single-book margin 6.82-6.85%, against
  3.92-3.96% at the best over and best under across the median 5-6 books (BetMGM alone 7.1%). Books
  disagree by a median SD of 1.0-1.1 points per prop, the size of a typical 2-4% "edge"; a low margin is
  not accuracy (FanDuel had the lowest margin and only consensus-level accuracy).
- +EV services pick fair sources, de-vig, weight, take EV >= 1-2% and stake a quarter Kelly
  (Outlier, Crazy Ninja Odds, Pikkit, Unabated). With these US books at 3 hours a services-style
  rule claimed +3.7% and +4.9% and returned +0.2% [-2.8%, +3.3%] and 0.0% [-6.7%, +6.8%]: most flags
  are noise in the fair price. Betting prices that beat the many-book average paid in football
  (Kaunitz, Zhong and Kreiner: 3.5% on 56,435 closing-odds bets, 8.5% on 265 real-money bets) until
  the accounts were limited, and one wrong long price can drive a published profit (Clegg and
  Cartlidge).

**CLV as the test**
- CLV per bet = price taken x margin-free closing probability of the same side at the same line - 1.
  If the close is efficient, expected ROI equals average CLV, and CLV varies about ten times less per
  bet (SD about 0.1 v about 1.00 for profit at evens); a consistent 5% CLV can show skill in "perhaps
  as few as just 50" bets (Buchdahl). It assumes a fair close (Whelan), is less settled for thin
  prop closes, and is what operators use to spot winners (Massachusetts memo). Betting returns are a
  poor measure of forecast accuracy (Wunderlich and Memmert 2020).
- The stored data cannot give CLV: one snapshot per game, and quote_time is the provider's refresh
  time. Repeated live snapshots suggest a further move with an SD of about 1.5-2.5 points per
  consensus prop after 3 hours, with 10-15% of lines changing. A free pilot found the market
  drifting slightly towards raw N1 (slope 0.044 [0.002, 0.088]), but N1's side at the best morning
  price had mean CLV of -2.9%. Report CLV as entry price against the 3-hour fair (line shopping, which does not carry
  over to UK books) plus drift (the model's information), separately for overs and unders.

**Sample sizes, power and multiple testing**
- At about 1.91 the per-bet profit SD is 0.95. For 80% power (one-sided 5%) a 3% edge needs about
  6,200 independent bets, 2% about 14,000 and 1% about 56,000; same-night and same-game correlation
  (design effect 1.31-1.68) raises the 2% figure to 18,000-24,000. CLV needs far fewer: with a
  per-bet CLV SD of 2.9-4.8%, a true 1% mean CLV needs about 50-140 bets (100-280 allowing for
  correlation) and 2% about 12-35 (25-70).
- The best of many rules is biased upwards: hence White's Reality Check, Hansen's SPA, a t hurdle of
  about 3 (Harvey, Liu and Zhu) or a deflated Sharpe ratio (Bailey and Lopez de Prado). Before this
  audit the project had tried 1,182 holdout rules plus 12 calibration, 215 distribution and 64 stack
  variants (the anchored rule's +4.1% [-2.8, +10.4] was the best of the 64); the Stage 2
  market-rules track added about 70 strategy-level ROI looks at 2025-26.

**Staking (fractional Kelly only after CLV is proven)**
- Kelly f* = edge / (decimal odds - 1): at 1.91 a 2% edge gives 2.2% of bankroll at full Kelly and
  0.55% at a quarter, for expected log growth of only about 2.2e-4 a bet. Twice Kelly gives zero
  growth; errors in the edge matter about ten times more than errors in variance (MacLean, Thorp and
  Ziemba); stakes should shrink when the edge is estimated with error (Baker and McHale); correlated
  simultaneous bets need joint treatment (Whitrow).
- With no demonstrated edge the Kelly stake is zero. If CLV later shows an edge e with standard
  error s, stake at most a quarter Kelly on a shrunk edge (about e x e^2 / (e^2 + s^2)), with a
  player's points and PRA, and same-game props, treated as one position under a per-game cap.

**The UK situation**
- No UK props in the data: The Odds API's prop coverage is "mainly limited to US sports and US
  bookmakers", and BetMGM is not in its UK list. UK books (bet365, Betfair Sportsbook, Sky Bet,
  William Hill, Unibet, 888sport) offer NBA and NFL props, but only by hand. Pinnacle left the UK in
  2014 and withdrew its licence application in 2018; US books require presence in a legal state.
- Margins: US NBA prop medians run 6.0-7.8% by book; a 2026 panel of 52 books puts props at 7.93% v
  4.77% for head-to-head; UK tips at 5/6 imply about 9.1% if both sides are 5/6 (unverified). At a
  single UK book a model would need to beat the fair price by about 4.5-5 points per side to break
  even, and the line-shopping part of any US EV does not carry over.
- Gubbing: in 2024, 643,779 of 14,923,840 active UK online betting accounts (4.31%) were
  commercially restricted, 62.17% of them by stake factoring, about 58% of which were cut below 10%
  of the normal maximum; 46.78% of restricted customers were in profit v 25.42% of all customers
  (Gambling Commission, July 2025). Massachusetts operators limit bettors who beat the close.
- Betfair's Expert Fee (from 6 January 2025) takes 20% of rolling 52-week gross profit between GBP
  25k and 100k and 40% above, and no usable player-prop exchange liquidity was confirmed. Winnings
  are not taxed (HMRC BIM22015); light-touch financial checks start at GBP 150 net deposits in a
  rolling 30 days. NBA tips fall between 00:00 and 03:30 UK time, and NFL inactives come about 90
  minutes before kick-off, after the stored 2-hour snapshot.

**Modelling practice**
- Serious systems take minutes and role from current injury and lineup news (DARKO, FiveThirtyEight,
  SaberSim), set recency and shrinkage per stat (Daly-Grafstein and Bornn; Engelmann), use the
  negative binomial for counts (Attard et al. 2023), treat opponent effects as small (Pomeroy), use
  the market as the prior (Kaunitz et al.; Spann and Skiera; Satopaa et al.) and, in the NFL, build
  team volume from market totals (nflfastR models; PFF; DraftSharks).

  limit. On the verifier's closely related version (698 and 215 bets) the two seasons did not differ

- Leave-one-out fair: no fit-season support (the 1,110 bets it adds made -0.02% in 2024-25), and for

- Injury-report absences: the market already prices them. L25 is a better standalone forecaster,

  dispersion changes were not adopted either (section 6.3).
- NFL fixes (starting QB from the prop, share shrinkage, M shapes, X1 weights): decision pending

## 9. How the paper record will be judged

1. **CLV first.** Per bet: recorded price x margin-free consensus probability of the same side at
   the same line in the last stored snapshot before tip-off, minus 1 (at least two books). Report the
   mean with a slate-block interval, the share positive and the lead time of that "close", overs and
   unders separately. A real 1-2% CLV shows within a few hundred bets.
2. **Minimum sample.** At least 50-100 tracked bets with a CLV before anything is called "working";
   D-044 sets the first formal judgement at 100.
3. **Profit is the long-run check, not the test.** A 2% edge needs about 14,000 independent bets for
   80% power (18,000-24,000 with same-night correlation): far longer than a season at the D-044
   rule's backtest rate (225 bets on 42 sample dates).
4. **Proposed decision rule (Stage 1 research).** Retire a rule if its mean CLV is at or below zero,
   or if the upper end of its interval is below half its mean claimed EV. If CLV is at least 1% with
   an interval above zero, keep paper-tracking and add hand-logged UK prices before anything else.
5. **Staking.** Nothing is staked until CLV is positive with an interval above zero; after that, at
   most a quarter Kelly on a shrunk edge with a per-game cap, and UK stake limits will bind first.
6. **One primary hypothesis.** `nba-anchored-ev2-mgm@D044` is judged on mean CLV above zero; leads
   are judged as a family with Holm. No rule changes after its first recorded run. Only nba-0.3
   editions published after the day's odds pull should feed it, so that their line factors are set.
7. **Capturing the close.** A near-tip (about 5 minutes) live snapshot for the game-markets holding
   a tracked bet costs about 8-10 credits a slate day, but needs a scheduler (none is installed),
   because NBA tips fall after midnight UK time. Until then, "close" means the last stored snapshot.

## 10. Caveats

- Every price here is a US research reference from The Odds API's us region. A UK customer cannot
  take any of them. BetMGM US is not BetMGM UK, and BetMGM is not in the provider's UK list.
- Snapshots are about 3 hours (NBA) and 2 hours (NFL) before the start, not the close. Lines keep
  moving (an SD of about 1.5-2.5 points per consensus prop, estimated from a few live games), and
  late news, including NFL inactives, arrives after the snapshot.
- The 2025-26 sample and NFL weeks 12-22 are not pristine. The only clean holdout is 2026-27, live.
- Best-price results assume accounts at several US books; with one or two accounts the margin is the
  single-book 7% or so, so a model needs an edge about 3 points larger than those backtests imply.
- Prop limits are low and winning accounts are limited quickly; capacity, not ROI, would bind first,
  and from October 2026 some markets are restricted (for example unders on short-minutes players).
- Historical injury flags cannot be replayed, so live eligibility is stricter than the backtests
  (99% of backtest bets were on players who played their team's previous game).
- Verification results are those recorded by the time stated above; pending rows may change.
