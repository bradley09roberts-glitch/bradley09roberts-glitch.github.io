Countries are ISO 3166-1 alpha-3, with the UK associations as ENG, SCO, WAL and NIR (as in
`reference/teams.csv`) and Kosovo as XKX. Team names are Wikipedia's display labels; the
article titles are in the teams file.

## Parsing rules

- **Kick-off.** Every page states that its times are CET/CEST "as listed by UEFA"; a page
  without that statement is refused. Times are converted with the Europe/Paris zone, so
  matchdays up to 24 October are UTC+2 and later ones UTC+1. Where a box also shows the
  venue's local time and UTC offset, the two must agree, or a warning is raised.
- **Regulation score.** Wikipedia lists the score after extra time. For a match that went to
  extra time, the regulation score is rebuilt from the goal minutes (90+x counts, 91 and later
  doesn't). It is kept only when the goal list adds up to the final score; otherwise the goals
  are left empty with an error. `extra_time` and `penalties` are flags only.
- **Extra time without a marker** (since 22 Oct, audit M14; sources do omit it). Outside the
  league phase a match counts as having gone to extra time when the box says so (`aet`,
  `{{aet}}`), when a goal is listed after the 90th minute (not 90+x), when it has a penalty
  score, or when the page's summary table marks the leg a.e.t. or on penalties (a warning, or
  an error if the regulation score can't be rebuilt). A tie must then go to extra time exactly
  when it is level after 90 minutes (over both legs; in the single-match preliminary round, in
  the match); where it doesn't, neither leg keeps a regulation score (`tie_inconsistent`, an
  error): the matches stay "played" with no score, so nothing is settled or fitted on them.
- **Not played** (since 22 Oct, audit H5). A walkover or awarded result is stored as
  `awarded` (with the awarded score if the box gives one; excluded from the models); an
  abandoned, postponed or suspended match as `postponed`, a cancelled one as `cancelled`, both
  without a score (a score at abandonment is not a result). Such a box is not a leg, so a
  replay keeps the leg number. A score the parser can't read is an error and the box is left
  out; while any such error stands, the loader deletes nothing.
- **Stage.** League phase: `League MD1` to `League MD8`. Qualifying: `Q1`, `Q2`, `Q3` or
  `Play-off`, then the path where the heading gives one (`CP` Champions Path, `LP` League
  Path, `MP` Main Path), then `leg 1` or `leg 2` (the first box of a pairing in a round is
  leg 1). UCL Q1 is all Champions Path but its heading doesn't say so, so it carries no path.
- **Neutral.** Set when the stadium line names a country other than the home team's (Shakhtar
  Donetsk at Stamford Bridge, Israeli and Belarusian clubs abroad), or by
  `venue_overrides.csv` (one row: Union Saint-Gilloise in Düsseldorf, from German Wikipedia;
  until 22 Oct this came from UEFA.com).
- **Names and flags.** Old flag codes still used on some pages are mapped (SLO, LAT, ROM).

## Known limits

- Played scores come from Wikipedia only, or, while Wikipedia can't be fetched, from scores
  entered by hand from news reports (`footy euro-import-results`, two independent publishers
  each, D-029), which a later Wikipedia load confirms or corrects. The goal-list,
  summary-table and tie checks above are internal to Wikipedia.
- The UEL and UECL qualifying pages were last edited in August; they are complete for the
  rounds they cover (every tie matches its summary), but a later correction would only
  arrive with a re-fetch.
- Fixtures further ahead may still move; re-fetch before each matchday
  (`uv run footy euro-update --fetch-wikipedia`). The fetch stops at the first HTTP 403 or 429.
- The knockout phase (from the February 2027 play-offs) has no page spec yet (audit M15).
