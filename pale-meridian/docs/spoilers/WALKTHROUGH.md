# ⚠️ SPOILERS — Complete walkthrough

> Developer/QA document: every solution, location and both endings. Coordinates are world block
> coordinates (x, y, z) and are identical in every world. Operators can jump anywhere with
> `/function palemeridian:admin/goto {poi:"<name>"}` (names in `tools/generated/poi.json`).

## Prologue — The Letter (the Landing, spawn 0 86 352)

1. **p.letter** — Use the noticeboard beside the waystation (-2 87 349). Read Tamsin's letter; choose
   *Take the letter and go* (you receive it as a book).
2. **p.camp** — Walk up the road to Tamsin's camp (16 88 336). Arriving completes it. The camp chest
   has bread, torches, 8 iron nuggets, 2 stone bricks, an iron chain, Tamsin's Lantern, a map, apples.
3. **p.chill** (optional, automatic) — stand in the fog holding a torch or lantern.
4. **p.lamp** — The broken lamp post (5 86 346) shows glowing ghosts: bottom to top **stone bricks,
   stone bricks, chain, lantern**. Craft a lantern from 8 iron nuggets + a torch. Any stone-brick,
   chain or lantern variant works. *Recovery:* the camp chest refills while the lamp is unbuilt.
5. **p.road** — A figure with a lantern appears on the road (12 86 300) and vanishes as you approach.
   Follow the lamp road north to Hollin's gate (107 67 154).

## Chapter 1 — Hollin, Unremembered

1. **c1.odile** — Talk to Odile at the lit lamp inside the gate (115 67 151); any path through the
   conversation ends with *"I'll listen."* Spawn moves to Hollin.
2. **c1.names** (0/4) — Walk into the **bakery** (145 68 113), the **hall** (93 68 115), the **well**
   in the square (120 68 118) and the **ferry steps** by the lake (78 66 78). Each plays an echo and
   gives a family name: **Dunn** (bakery/bread), **Pell** (well/water), **Marsh** (hall/words),
   **Tarn** (ferry/home).
3. **c1.round** — Climb the bell tower (belfry ~120 83 96). Four bells carry the family names:
   Dunn (north, 120 85 94), Pell (east, 122 85 96), Marsh (south, 120 85 98), Tarn (west, 118 85 96).
   The Round (painted in the Hall; Odile hums it): *"Bread for the morning, water for noon, words for
   the evening, the ferry for home."* → ring **Dunn, Pell, Marsh, Tarn**. A wrong bell resets.
   The hatches above the belfry open.
4. **c1.lamp** — In the cradle chest (123 87 99) is the **Stillglass Lamp** (pearlescent froglight).
   At the top (120 88 96): lamp in the centre, **4 copper grates** around it (any oxidation; stonecut
   from copper blocks), **2 chains** above. *Recovery:* the chest refills the lamp if it is neither
   built nor carried; bells repair themselves.
5. **c1.surge** — *Hold the Light*, in the square: the four plaza lamps go dark and Watchers appear
   (2 + 2 per extra player, max 7; −1 on Story, +2 on Hard). Keep them in view, use the *Relight*
   prompts on the four lamps. Win when all four are lit and at least 30 s have passed. Everyone
   leaving for 15 s resets it (10 s cooldown).
6. **Outcome** — Hollin clears; Odile Marsh remembers: the Deepcut fell and the Meridian's light went
   dark the same night; Tamsin went east. Mirelle gives bread daily; Jory mentions his sister Agnes.
7. **c1.home** (optional) — place a bed inside the Surveyor's Rest (134 68 132).

## Chapter 2 — The Heartwood (Aldercross)

1. **c2.arrive** — Follow the east road to the orchard gate (302 78 -34).
2. **c2.brannoc** — Talk to Brannoc (312 78 -40); end with *"I'll go."* (Before Chapter 2 he only
   tells you to come back when Hollin's bells ring.)
3. **c2.memories** (0/3) — Walk into the **apiary** (~314 80 -73), the **cider press** barn
   (~346 82 -26) and the shade of the **Brothers' Tree** (~320 82 -4). In the press loft, the
   *Tamsin's notes* prompt (344 82 -23) gives her **Field Notes** (needed in Chapter 3).
4. **c2.hearts** (0/3) — The Heartwood (358 90 -14) has three creaking hearts: one in the trunk
   (7 blocks up, inside the wood) and one in each root arm (east and west, 6 blocks out, 1 up).
   Hitting a heart-bound Watcher shows resin particles flowing to its heart. Break all three; the
   pale roots in the windmill disappear.
5. **c2.lamp** — Brannoc gives Col's Stillglass Lamp. Windmill cap (356 93 -46): lamp in the
   centre, **4 lanterns** on the corners, **2 honeycomb blocks** north and south. Honeycomb: 8 in
   the apiary shed chest (refills while this step is active). Ladder up the inside west wall.
6. **Outcome** — Aldercross clears; bees, cornflowers and dandelions return. Brannoc: Col died in the
   Deepcut with ten others; the Keeper, **Hesper Vane**, sent them past the safe seam. He gives Col's
   carved apple token if you haven't found it, and a daily gift afterwards.

## Chapter 3 — The Glassworks (and the Deepcut)

1. **c3.arrive** — North road to the Glassworks gate (44 70 -284).
2. **c3.log** — Foreman's log in the office (36 70 -318). Procedure: **bellows UP, flue DOWN, damper
   UP, then 8 coal or charcoal**. Last entries: Keeper's orders past the blue seam; "Eleven."
3. **c3.tamsin** — Mine mouth (24 70 -320) → adit north → side tunnel east at z −338 → assay room →
   stairs north → upper gallery. Tamsin sits by a dead lamp (56 82 -362). Two heart-bound Watchers
   haunt the adit (hearts in pale-oak posts at 22 71 -352 and 26 71 -376).
4. **c3.remind** (0/3) — Show her: **the letter** (copy at the Landing noticeboard), **her Field
   Notes** (Aldercross loft), **her theodolite** (office chest 31 70 -316; refills). She restores and
   explains: the fog is attention; the Keeper struck the Deepcut from the Long Chart.
5. **c3.memorial** — Follow the adit north past the blue seam (signs "SAFE LIMIT" / "KEEPER'S ORDERS:
   CONTINUE — T.V."), dig through the collapse (z −393…−398) into the Last Gallery (~24 74 -411):
   eleven pale figures facing eleven blank plaques. Echo: Hesper ordering the Deepcut struck from the
   Chart; Tobin promising the roof would hold. **Twist: the Keeper struck her own son's name.**
6. **c3.kiln** — Kiln Three control panel (levers at 15 71 -304/-303/-302): wall levers point up when
   off → **bellows off (up), flue on (down), damper off (up)**. At the start the bellows is already
   right, so only the flue and the damper need flipping (flipping all three is wrong). Put **8 coal/charcoal** in the firebox
   barrel (12 70 -298). The hatch chest (12 70 -295) now holds a Stillglass Lamp and the **Lens Heart**
   (heart of the sea). Wrong settings: smoke and a hint. Levers repair themselves.
7. **c3.lamp** — Kiln tower (ladder inside, top at 0 98 -308): lamp in the centre, **4 glass blocks**
   around (any glass), **2 iron bars** stacked above. The workshop supply chest (40 70 -302) refills
   glass and bars while this step is active.
8. **c3.surge** — *The Collapse*, kiln yard (24 70 -296): four candle clusters on pedestals; relight
   with the prompts or flint and steel within **120 s**; Watchers come from the mine mouth (3 + 2 per
   extra player, max 8). Failing or leaving resets it (10 s cooldown). **Outcome:** the Glassworks
   clears; the Deepcut stays in the Pall; Tamsin goes to Hollin (112 67 128).

## Chapter 4 — The Meridian

1. **c4.crossing** — Before this chapter the island folds you back to the Hollin jetty. Now: take a boat
   from the Hollin jetty (72 64 72; two boats moored) or walk the causeway north from the south shore
   (x −10, z 100 → 16). The causeway has a 6-block gap at z 52–57: bridge it (optional
   **c4.causeway**, lights the causeway lamps) or swim/row round it. Reaching the island completes it.
2. **c4.keeper** — Hesper in the dark Chart Room (-4 68 -18). The Long Chart mosaic has the Deepcut
   painted white. Her story; end with *"I have to light it."*
3. **c4.lens** — Climb the tower stair (28 steps) to the Lens Gallery; use *The Great Lens* prompt
   (-14 96 -14) with the Lens Heart. Companions gather in the Chart Room.
4. **c4.unlooked** — The final encounter on the gallery. **The Unlooked**: a creaking at 2.5× scale,
   180 HP + 90 per extra player (Story 110 + 60, Hard 240 + 110), attack 5.
   - Moves only when unobserved; hit it while you watch it.
   - Every 20 s (12 s below ⅔ health; +6 s on Story) it telegraphs (3 s of smoke) and snuffs one of
     the four relay lamps. With two or more dark it heals 3 HP/s: relight with the *Relight* prompts.
   - Below ⅔: Watchers climb in every 10 s (cap = players + 1).
   - Below ⅓: every 15 s it steps behind a random player (darkness pulse unless disabled).
   - Everyone away for 10 s: full reset (10 s cooldown). The Keeper's quarters (-27 70 0) have beds.
   **Win:** a beacon lights the Lens; the Long Chart glows.
5. **c4.chart** — *The Long Chart* prompt in the Chart Room (2 68 -20). Hear the others first
   (Tamsin: truth; Brannoc: the names; Odile: the living; Hesper: mercy). Choose, then confirm.

## Endings

|---|---|---|
| World | every district clears, including the Deepcut and the lake; clear skies (Pall clock 5000) | all but the Deepcut clear; a faint haze remains (clock 6000) |
| Deepcut | plaques show the eleven names, candles and mine lamps lit, the figures are gone | fog stays, the eleven figures remain |
| Hesper | tends a small garden at the mine mouth (20 70 -316), restored | keeps the mine mouth, still pale |
| Extra | dawn is set; if all eleven keepsakes were found: *Every Name, Spoken* | if all eleven were found: *Kept Safe* |

Then: credits, the **Keeper's Glass** for every player (also given to later joiners), *Survey
Complete*, free play with epilogue lines for Tamsin, Brannoc, Odile, Mirelle, Jory and Hesper.

## The drowned chapel (optional, -340 64 70)

Entering starts **s.fen**. The Vow of the First Keeper is on the lectern. Levers on the south wall:
the verse says *"Open the north to let the fen breathe. Shut the middle against the lake. Open the
south to send it home."* → **north ON, middle OFF, south ON**. The crypt drains and its glass window
breaks; climb down the ladder shaft in the south-east corner. Crypt: Mae Ostrander's hymn sheet and
the **Ferryman's Oar** (wooden shovel, Efficiency II).
