# Expeditions: the Nether, the stronghold and the End

The friends prepare everything for beating the game on their own (see Sage's plan), but the dangerous trips are
**expeditions you lead**. Friends who follow you go through portals with you and come back with you. In the Nether
they barter with piglins, help hunt blazes and pick up what is worth taking home. Scout finds the stronghold by
throwing eyes of ender. In the stronghold the friends fill the End portal, and in the End they shoot the crystals,
open the caged ones and strike the dragon when it lands. Friends never take on the dragon by themselves: in Hardcore
that would only make graves.

## Your party

Each player has their own party. A friend is in one party at most (adding them to yours takes them out of another
player's).

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/friends party` or `/friends party list` | Who is in your party, what each is doing and where, and the places expeditions have found (Nether portals, fortresses, the stronghold, the End portal, the camp's portal) |
| `/friends party add <name>` | Adds a friend (one of the nine or a newcomer) to your party |
| `/friends party remove <name>` | Takes a friend out of your party |
| `/friends party go` | Everyone in your party within 128 blocks follows you. Members near the camp stop at the supply chest to pack first |
| `/friends party home` | Everyone in your party goes back to work at the camp. A friend in another dimension goes home through the portal they came in by |

All of these work without cheats and hand out nothing. `/friends follow <name>` still works for single friends too.

### Packing

When you send your party off with `go`, members within 48 blocks of the supply chest stop there first and take:

- food for the trip, up to **6 pieces**;
- a **potion of fire resistance**, if the chest has one;
- and, carried by one member for the whole party, what the step of Sage's plan in hand calls for: **up to 8 gold
  ingots** to barter with (while the plan is on the Nether steps), **up to 12 eyes of ender** (once the stronghold is
  found and until the End portal is open), or **up to 32 cobblestone, dirt or netherrack** to pillar up to a caged
  crystal (once the End portal is open).

They only take what is in the chest. When they are back at work, the deposit job puts whatever is left back.

## Through portals

- **Coming along.** When you go through a portal (into the Nether, back out, into the End, or out of the End after the
  dragon), every friend following you who was within **32 blocks** of where you left comes through with you and
  appears on a safe spot beside you. One of them says so.
- **Left behind.** A follower who was further away remembers the portal you used, walks to it and comes through after
  you. While nobody is near them, their part of the world keeps running (a roaming ticket, as for trips) so they do
  not freeze half way.
- **The way back.** Every friend remembers the portal they came in by, and the camp remembers every crossing players
  have made, both ways. Friends use these to find their way.
- **On their own.** A friend in the Nether or the End whose leader has gone (logged out, died, or went home without
  them), or one you told to stay there and left alone, waits by the portal they came in by for **3 minutes**, then
  goes home through it and back to work. A follower at home whose leader is unreachable goes back to work after the
  same wait.
- **Back to work.** `/friends work <name>` (or `/friends party home`) on a friend in another dimension sends them home
  through the portal. Away from the camp's dimension no job ever runs, so nobody tries to walk to a camp in another
  world.
- **No accidental trips.** Friends never step through a portal on their own: one wandering through the camp's portal
  stays where they are. They only cross when following you, or when going home.
- **The End.** In the End a follower more than 16 blocks behind you catches up beside you instead of walking (if
  catching up is on, `followTeleportDistance`): the way to you there is often a narrow bridge over the void.

## The Nether portal at camp

Once Sage's plan reaches **Nether ready** (10 obsidian and a flint and steel in the camp), the builder (Oak first,
anyone in his place) builds a portal at the camp: a frame of 10 obsidian, 4 wide and 5 high, with cobblestone corners,
on a proper building site, from real materials in the chest. Then he fetches the flint and steel and lights it.
Everyone on the server hears that it is lit, with its coordinates.

- It is built and lit once. A portal that goes out later is yours to light again.
- If you already go through a portal of your own at the camp, the friends do not build another.
- A lit portal in the overworld can let zombified piglins through, as any portal does.

## In the Nether, with you

Friends following you stay within a few blocks of you, and make themselves useful:

- **Picking things up.** Ender pearls, potions, string, obsidian, crying obsidian, quartz, blaze rods and powder, nether
  wart, glowstone dust, magma cream, ghast tears, gold and fire charges lying within 8 blocks. Never anything you threw,
  anything right next to you (you may be about to take it), or anything in lava or fire.
- **Bartering.** A friend carrying gold ingots tosses one to a calm, grown piglin near you about every 15 seconds
  while the camp wants what piglins trade: ender pearls or obsidian for the plan, or the camp has little string,
  quartz or fire resistance. Never a piglin that is angry, already admiring gold, or that you have just hit. The
  piglin throws its trade towards you; the friends pick up the useful bits.
- **Piglins are left alone.** Friends treat a piglin as a threat only once it goes for you or a friend. Calm piglins
  are no longer attacked (before this, Aegis would have started a fight with every piglin in sight).
- **Blazes.** Archers shoot blazes with the bow (the combat update), and a blaze a friend kills drops its rod because
  the kill is credited to you.
- **Nether wart and soul sand.** While the camp has fewer than 24 wart or 12 soul sand, friends harvest nether wart
  within 5 blocks, and the soul sand it grows on, up to 12 wart and 6 soul sand each. Never beside anything you
  built (a fortress's own bricks do not count). Back at camp, they go into the chest for the brewing.
- **Places.** The portal you came out of and any fortress you walk into with friends are remembered
  (`/friends party` lists them), and one friend calls the fortress out.
- **Safety.** Friends keep out of lava and fire as they always do, drink a fire resistance potion when burning (the
  combat update's emergency healing), and stay close.

## Finding the stronghold (Scout)

When Sage's plan reaches **Stronghold found** (the camp has its eyes of ender), Scout sets off one morning:

1. She takes up to 3 eyes of ender (and a few blocks, torches, cobblestone and food) from the chest.
2. She walks out past the camp's edge and throws an eye. It is a real eye of ender: you can watch it fly. She notes
   the way it flew and picks it up again if it did not break (it survives four times in five).
3. She walks **200 blocks** across that line and throws another. Where the two lines cross is the stronghold. If the
   lines are nearly parallel (it is very far away), she walks across again and throws a third.
4. She walks there (it can be over a thousand blocks: she shelters at night like anyone far from camp and carries on
   in the morning) and builds a small marker over the spot: three cobblestone with a torch on top, only into air,
   never within 6 blocks of anything you built.
5. She comes home and tells everyone where it is ("x 1204, z -388"). The stronghold is added to the camp's places and
   the plan's **Stronghold found** step is done.

She never digs down. Hurt, or hungry with nothing to eat, she turns back and reports what she knows. If the way is
blocked (an ocean), she reports the spot unmarked. Nobody else does this while Scout is about; without her, another
friend stands in. Trips must be on (`allowTrips`).

If you find the stronghold first and walk into it with friends following you, the step is done too.

## The End portal

In the stronghold's portal room, friends following you who carry eyes of ender set one in each empty frame they can
reach, one at a time (each eye really used). When all twelve frames hold an eye they open the portal, just as setting
the last eye does for you, and the plan's **End portal open** step is done. They change nothing but the frames and
the opening, and only with you close by. If you fill the last frame yourself, the step is done all the same.

## The dragon

Friends who come through to the End with you help in the fight:

- **The crystals.** The team works out, for each end crystal, a spot on the island's ground from which a full draw of
  the bow clears everything on the way (the tower's own edge, its cage), and archers walk there and shoot. Nobody may
  stand within 12 blocks of a crystal when it is shot (its blast throws people about that far): the friends hold fire
  while anyone, you included, is that close. A crystal a friend's arrow sets off cannot hurt you or any friend.
- **The caged crystals.** For a crystal in an iron cage, one friend with a pickaxe, plenty of cobblestone, dirt or
  netherrack and good health pillars up beside its tower (jumping and placing blocks under themselves), breaks the
  cage's bars within reach, and comes back down taking the pillar with them. Then the archers can see in. Hurt, they
  come straight down. A climb that did not work is tried again a minute later from another side, four times at most.
- **The dragon in flight.** Archers shoot it while it flies, leading it a little, with arrows of the friends' own
  that can hurt it (see Settings below).
- **The dragon on the portal.** When it lands on the portal, a friend calls it out, and friends with a sword or axe
  strike it (the head when they can reach it). Arrows do nothing to a perched dragon, as for you.
- **Safety.** Friends get out of the dragon's breath, choose shooting spots on firm ground away from the island's
  edge, and never use beds. Nobody chases the dragon like a mob. If a friend is knocked into the void there is nothing
  anyone can do: this is Hardcore.
- **Victory.** When the dragon dies, every friend in the End cheers, the Unity bond grows by **100**, everyone on the
  server hears who was there, and Sage's plan reaches its last step. When you go through the exit portal, the friends
  near you come home with you.

## Settings (`config/hardcorefriends.json`)

| Setting | Default | What it does here |
|---|---|---|
| `friendsFollowThroughPortals` | `true` | Off: friends never come through portals with you (those already in another dimension still go home on their own) |
| `progressionGoals` | `true` | Off: no portal at camp, no stronghold search and no packing for the plan's steps (the rest still works) |
| `followTeleportDistance` | `48` | Catching up with you when far behind; in the End it is at most 16 (0 turns catching up off everywhere) |
| `maxRoamingFriends` | `3` | Also limits how many friends left behind in another dimension keep moving while nobody is near them |
| `allowTrips` | `true` | Off: Scout does not go looking for the stronghold |

The friends' blows and arrows on the dragon use two damage types of the mod's own (`hardcorefriends:friend_strike`
and `hardcorefriends:friend_arrow`), added to the game's `minecraft:always_hurts_ender_dragons` tag (and the arrow to
`minecraft:is_projectile`). In 26.3 the dragon only takes damage from players or from damage types in that tag.

## Honest limits

Nothing here has been run in the game yet. It compiles, and was checked by reading it through against the game's
own code, but none of it has been played.

- **The dragon fight is the riskiest thing friends do.** The climber up a pillar can be knocked off by the dragon,
  and a player shooting a caged crystal while a friend is beside it will blow the friend up (the friends' own shots
  wait for everyone to be clear; yours do not). Friends striking the perched dragon stand close to its breath; they
  step out of it, but only once it is on the ground. Friends who fall into the void are lost.
- **Shooting is approximate.** The aim allows for the arrow's drop at a full draw, and the whole flight is checked
  for blocks, but the dragon is fast: many arrows at it miss. A crystal with no clear shot from the ground (a cage
  still closed on every side the friends can reach) is left to you. Arrows at the dragon do a quarter of their damage
  unless they hit the head, as for you.
- **Coming along needs room.** A friend comes through only onto a safe spot (firm ground, no lava or fire) within a few
  blocks of you. Arriving on a ledge over a lava lake, they try for two seconds and then walk to the portal and come
  through after you, landing by the portal you came out of.
- **Left behind, far away.** A friend left in another dimension keeps moving only while there is a roaming ticket to
  give (3 by default, shared with trips). Without one, or once the world is closed, they wait where they are until a
  player comes by again. A friend who cannot reach the portal for 90 seconds (no path) finds their way across from
  where they stand. A friend going home whose portal on the far side is not loaded comes out at the camp instead (if
  the camp is in that dimension and running); otherwise they wait and try again.
- **No camp, no way.** A friend in the Nether or the End who never came through a portal (put there some other way)
  and has no camp to go home to waits there.
- **Following through the End exit portal.** After the credits you come back at your spawn point; friends who were
  near you at the exit portal come along. Friends further off walk to the exit portal and come through to your spawn
  point.
- **The stronghold search is long.** A stronghold is usually over a thousand blocks away. Scout's trip can take two
  or three days, with nights in a shelter; it is given up after three days. Scout only throws from the overworld, uses
  up the eyes that break, and is a stand-in friend's job without her. The marker is built on the surface over the
  spot, which can be off by a few blocks.
- **Bartering is simple.** The friends toss gold and pick up what lands near them. They do not choose a trade, and you
  may get to the piglin's throw first.
- **Places are the expeditions' own list** (shown by `/friends party`, 64 at most). Only the stronghold is also added to
  the camp's points of interest, because those have no dimension.
- **Parties are kept by name for friends who are far away**; a newcomer renamed in between shows their old name.
- **Commands and trust.** `/friends party go` and `home` give orders like `/friends follow` and `work`. If the
  several-players update adds trust checks to orders, these commands should check the same.

## Corrections to GUIDE.md and DESIGN.md

These existing statements change with this update and should be updated when it is merged:

- **DESIGN 4, Threats:** a piglin counts as a threat only once it goes for a player or a friend (before, every piglin
  did, so armed friends would have attacked calm piglins). The ender dragon is never a threat to chase: the expedition
  goals fight it.
- **DESIGN 4, goals table:** add `CageClimbGoal` (−1), `EndHazardGoal` (1), `PackGoal` and `DragonFightGoal` (3),
  `TravelGoal`, `FillPortalGoal` and `NetherHelpGoal` (4). `FollowLeaderGoal` catches up at 16 blocks in the End.
- **DESIGN 3, Modes:** FOLLOW now goes through portals with the leader; a friend in another dimension with nobody to
  follow goes home after 3 minutes.
- **DESIGN 7, World editing:** add the `EXPEDITION` reason (see the Nether, stronghold and End rules above).
- **DESIGN 8, stage 3:** add the optional Nether portal (built for Sage's plan).
- **`CompanionEntity.homePos`:** away from the camp's dimension it is the leader (when following) or the portal the
  friend came in by, so falling back leads there rather than to the camp's coordinates in the wrong world.
